# Assistive AI for the Visually Impaired - Simple Explanation

## What is this app?
Imagine giving blind or visually impaired people a helpful friend who can see the world through their phone camera and tell them what's around them - that's what this app does.

## How does it work?
1. **You point your phone camera** at what's in front of you
2. **The app looks at what the camera sees** in real-time (like a live video)
3. **It tells you important things** through speech, sounds, or vibrations in your headphones
4. **It works like a helpful assistant** that whispers useful information in your ear

## What can it do?
- **Find objects and obstacles**: Tells you if there's a chair, table, wall, or pole ahead
- **Read text**: Reads signs, labels, menus, money, and documents out loud
- **Describe scenes**: Can tell you what a room or street looks like in simple words
- **Warn about dangers**: Alerts you quickly about stairs, holes, low branches, or moving objects
- **Help with money**: Identifies different bills and coins
- **Work offline**: Most important features work even without internet

## Who is it for?
People who are blind or have low vision who want to be more independent in their daily lives - walking around, shopping, cooking, traveling, or just moving around their home.

## Why is it special?
- **Uses your existing phone** - no special equipment needed
- **Focuses on safety first** - warns about dangers quickly (under 1/4 second)
- **Works privately** - most processing happens on your phone, not in the cloud
- **Simple to use** - designed to work with voice commands and headphones, no screen needed
- **Always improving** - gets better over time based on user feedback

## Real-life examples
- **At home**: Find the microwave, read food labels, avoid walking into furniture
- **Outside**: Find crosswalks, read store signs, avoid low tree branches or construction
- **Shopping**: Identify money, read product labels, find items on shelves
- **Traveling**: Navigate train stations, find doors, read departure boards

## The big idea
Instead of needing expensive special devices, this app turns the smartphone you already own into a helpful visual assistant - giving you more confidence and independence to move through the world safely.
